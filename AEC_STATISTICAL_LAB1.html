Discrete Probability Distributions

Interactive laboratory application for studying Binomial, Poisson and Geometric probability distributions. Enter parameters, calculate important statistical measures, evaluate probabilities and visualize the distribution.

Binomial Distribution

Models the number of successes in a fixed number of independent trials.

Poisson Distribution

Models the number of events occurring in a fixed interval.

Geometric Distribution

Models the trial number on which the first success occurs.

Binomial Distribution

The Binomial Distribution gives the probability of getting exactly x successes in n independent trials, where each trial has probability p of success.

P(X = x) = C(n,x) px(1-p)n-x

Poisson Distribution

The Poisson Distribution models the number of times an event occurs in a fixed interval when the average rate is λ.

P(X = x) = e−λ λx / x!

Geometric Distribution

The Geometric Distribution gives the probability that the first success occurs on the x-th trial, where each trial has success probability p.

P(X = x) = (1 − p)x−1p,   x = 1,2,3,...
